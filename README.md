
# 🔥 YouthHub Check-In & Roster

A lightweight, offline-first **Youth Ministry Check-In & Student Management Progressive Web App (PWA)** designed for phones and tablets. Inspired by classic youth ministry tools, YouthHub keeps all student records, photos, and signed permission forms stored **100% locally on your device** with easy JSON backups to Google Drive.

## ✨ Key Features
- **Local Leader Account & PIN Lock:** Quick setup stored privately on the device with a one-tap lock screen.
- **Fast Event & Service Check-In:** Create dated events (*Midweek Service*, *Sunday Gathering*, *Special Event / Camp*), filter by grade (6th–12th), tap cards to check students in, or add first-time guests on the spot.
- **Complete Student Profiles:** Track contact info, texting permissions, school, grade, photo, parent/guardian contacts, allergies/medical notes, social media photo release status, and upload/view **signed PDF permission forms**.
- **Smart Follow-Up Alerts:**
  - **Upcoming Birthdays:** Automatically highlights student birthdays within the next 14 days with one-tap text/copy greetings.
  - **2 Consecutive Missed Midweek Services:** Flags students who miss two midweek gatherings in a row with suggested follow-up actions.
- **Offline Storage & Google Drive Backups:** Saves data locally in the browser (`IndexedDB` / `localStorage`) and exports/restores complete `.json` backup files you can save directly to your Google Drive.

## 📱 How to Install on Your Phone or Tablet
1. Open the live GitHub Pages link in **Chrome (Android)** or **Safari (iPhone/iPad)**.
2. Tap the browser menu (**⋮** on Android or **Share** on iOS) and choose **"Add to Home Screen"** or **"Install App"**.
3. Launch YouthHub from your home screen like a native app!
